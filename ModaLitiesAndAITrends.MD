# AI Modalities and Trends

## Overview: This matrix highlights how AI capability evolves across different data modalities as we move from simple recognition to generation, reasoning, and full autonomy.

The progression is:
1. Discriminative AI: Detect, classify, and predict.
2. Generative AI: Create new content from patterns.
3. Agentic & Reasoning AI: Plan, use tools, and complete multi-step workflows.
4. Autonomous & Embodied AI: Act in the physical or digital world with minimal human intervention.

### Core Paradigms Summary
* **Discriminative AI (Pattern Recognition):** Analyzes existing data to classify, detect, or predict outcomes based on trained patterns.
* **Generative AI (Content Creation):** Synthesizes completely new media, text, audio, or video based on probabilistic pattern generation.
* **Agentic & Reasoning AI (Logic & Tool Use):** Executes multi-step workflows, uses external tools (APIs, web browsers, compilers), and applies System-2 step-by-step thinking to solve complex technical problems.
* **Autonomous & Embodied AI (Physical & Digital Control):** Integrates Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models directly into software agents or robotic hardware (humanoids, self-driving cars, drones) to interact continuously with the real world without human intervention.
